Privacy Policy

Varsity Football Coach · Updated November 13, 2026 · Terms of Use

Varsity Football Coach is a football coaching game. This policy explains what the game does with information. The short version: the game keeps everything on your device, has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and we do not collect personal information from you.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in the game is saved only on your phone or in your browser:

  • your career saves, school, staff, uniform and settings
  • any player names you type or scan into the roster import
  • your purchase status and daily gift streak

We have no server that receives this. If you delete a career in the Careers screen, or delete the app, it is gone, and we cannot recover it.

Roster names and photos

You can type, paste or photograph a roster sheet to put real names on your team. Photos are read on your device by text recognition built into the app. The photo is not uploaded and is not saved.

Imported names are marked as real and are hidden by default (shown as jersey numbers) when you share a newspaper recap as an image.

Only enter names of people you have the right to enter. If the players are minors, get permission from them and their parents or guardians.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your card or Apple ID details.

To confirm what you own, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app identifier and your App Store purchase receipts. It does not receive your name, your roster, or anything you create in the game. Its privacy policy is at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Apple's privacy policy is at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

What you choose to share

The game can create links, codes and images that you send to friends through the app you choose (Messages, email and so on). What they contain:

  • Friend league invite: the league name and a random number used to build the schedule.
  • Friend league team card: your coach name, school name and colors, player ratings and jersey numbers, and game plan. Never player names.
  • Challenge link: your coach name, your school, your first-season record and a random number.
  • Newspaper image: the recap you chose to share.

Once you send something, it is in the hands of the person and the app you sent it to. Only share what you are comfortable with.

Notifications and sensors

If you turn on the Friday reminder, the app schedules a notification on your device. It does not use a server. The game uses your camera only if you choose to scan a roster photo. It uses vibration for big plays.

Children

The game has no accounts, chat, ads or social network, and it does not ask for personal information from anyone, including children. It can be played without sharing anything.

Your choices

You can delete any career in the Careers screen, delete the app to remove everything stored on the device, and turn off notifications in your phone's Settings. Your purchases belong to your Apple ID and can be managed in your Apple account settings.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app with an updated date at the top.
